## An inbox that thinks along

Categories like "In progress" and "Waiting for reply", rules for the whole team, search like in
Gmail. Send later or snooze a mail until it's due – and a warning when a sender only pretends to be
your bank. Light or dark, as you like it.

## And why all of it is better off with you

</div>

<div class="showcase-cards" markdown>
<div markdown>
**🇪🇺 Your data stays with you**

On your server, in Europe or in your own office. No US company reading along. GDPR without the
headache.
</div>
<div markdown>
**💶 No cost per head**

Ten people cost as much as one. You pay for the server, not for every user.
</div>
<div markdown>
**🧩 One app instead of five**

Mail, calendar, tasks, chat, phone and documents with one sign-in.
</div>
<div markdown>
**📦 Moving in included**

Mail, contacts and appointments come along from Microsoft 365, Gmail, GMX or Outlook.
</div>
<div markdown>
**🔌 Outlook and iPhone keep working**

Whoever prefers Outlook, Thunderbird or the iPhone's Mail app can keep using it.
</div>
<div markdown>
**🤖 AI, if you want it**

Claude and other assistants look up customers, mails and appointments and write drafts. They never
send or delete.
</div>
<div markdown>
**🔐 Secure out of the box**

Sign-in with a second factor, encrypted mail and encrypted backups.
</div>
<div markdown>
**🔓 Open source, no lock-in**

Open source code and open standards. You can take everything with you at any time.
</div>
</div>
